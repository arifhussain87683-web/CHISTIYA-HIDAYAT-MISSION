CHISTIYA HIDAYAT MISSION — v1.0 Accounts Foundation

New:
- Accounts overview with total income, total expenses, net balance and entry count.
- Cashbook ledger for fee payment income and expense voucher outflows.
- Fee receipts post to Accounts only after invoice allocation succeeds.
- Expense vouchers post to Accounts after the expense record is saved.
- Ledger entries are linked to their source records and protected against duplicate source posting.

Database step required:
- Run CHISTIYA_HIDAYAT_MISSION_Accounts_Migration_v1.0.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor.
- The migration creates one new table and indexes and enables admin-only RLS.
- It does not drop, truncate, reset, or alter existing tables or records.

Limitations:
- No automatic import/backfill of old receipts or expenses.
- No manual donation/other-income entry, bank account reconciliation, or multi-account transfers yet.
- No full production build or live Supabase integration test was available in this packaging environment.
- If a source transaction saves but ledger posting fails, reconcile before retrying to avoid duplicate source transactions.
